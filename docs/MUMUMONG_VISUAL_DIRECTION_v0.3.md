## Components and motion

- Use warm off-white backgrounds, large readable type, generous spacing, soft pill actions, rounded surfaces and restrained translucent glass over color. Avoid stacking many glass cards or hiding copy beneath blur.
- Reserve the smooth status orb and moving border highlight for active dream input/recording and real generation-job stages. No orb on the home or passive archive screen.
- Remove dot, pixel-grid and ASCII treatments from current app screens and the PDF cover. Dream clarity is text, not a dot rating.
- Motion is atmospheric, not a progress claim. Job labels follow actual state. Reduced-motion settings receive a stable still frame.

The attached MetalForge project is a SwiftUI/Metal shader reference. The cross-platform Flutter implementation reinterprets its moving light and blue-white ramp rather than importing the magenta shader or a React component.
